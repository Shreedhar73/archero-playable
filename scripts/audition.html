Sound audition

Dev only: not in the build. The first tap unlocks the context exactly as the game's first touch does. Every button goes through GameAudio, so what plays here is what plays in the ad, cadence gate included. Knobs: MASTER and each entry of SFX in src/audio/audio.ts.

The game ships no background music: two synthesized beds were heard here and rejected. This is the AR scanner creative's own tune, at its own levels (0.11 under a 0.8 master), on a context of its own — it is not in the game. Run it under the combat loop to judge whether any bed belongs.

Twenty seconds of combat: a bow every 0.8 s, most arrows landing, a kill every 2.5 s, a hit taken every 6 s. The density is what the earlier versions were rejected for, not any one sound.
