Sound audition

Dev only: not in the build. The first tap unlocks the context and starts the bed, exactly as the game's first touch does. Everything in the first two sections goes through GameAudio, so what plays here is what plays in the ad, cadence gate included. Knobs in src/audio/audio.ts: MASTER, MUSIC, STEP_GAIN, each entry of SFX, and the LEAD / BASS_* tables.

Layers

The bed and the footsteps were built from measurements of the real game's recording; each has its own switch so it can be judged with and without the rest.

Effects, one at a time

Twenty seconds of play

Alternates two seconds of running (footsteps, no shooting; the hero only shoots standing still) with two seconds of standing (a bow every 0.8 s, most arrows landing, a kill every other stand), over the bed. Judge the density, not any one sound.

Measured effects, not in the game

The shot, hit and death as the recording measures them, on a context of their own, so the game's versions can be compared by ear before anything is swapped. Shot: 620 Hz for 15 ms then a 300 to 570 Hz rise, 100 ms, peak −10.6 dBFS. Hit: a 9 kHz ring, −20 dB after 130 to 350 ms, peak −8.4. Death: a low thump to −20 dB in 200 ms with a 5 to 7 kHz sparkle after it.
